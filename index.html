One local rack.
Many sovereign intelligences.

A new Ready S.E.T. Cooperative website centred on a Dunwich GB200-class rack example, Indigenous and non-Indigenous cultural collaboration, local models and a living digital twin of the islands. S.E.T. means Sustainable Employment and Training.

A simple boundary

The rack is capacity, not authority

Sovereignty was never ceded. A computer neither grants it nor defines it.

The machine may host local models, a digital twin, a media studio and scientific simulations. It does not become the owner of Country, culture, memory or identity.

Every person arrives with their own centre. First Nations sovereignty also carries collective, cultural and Country-based authority that a cooperative, company or model does not absorb.

A node rather than a centre

The Dunwich example can remain local while connecting into a much larger constellation.
