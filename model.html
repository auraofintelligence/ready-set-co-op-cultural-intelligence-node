Plural intelligence

A different response to artificial intelligence dominance

One universal model can make cultural difference look like noise. A federation can let difference remain useful.

A United States executive order uses the phrase “global artificial intelligence dominance”. This project explores another pattern: many local intelligences that can cooperate without being absorbed into one national or corporate centre.

Interoperability can remain possible while culture, memory, keys, model behaviour and the right to disconnect stay local.
