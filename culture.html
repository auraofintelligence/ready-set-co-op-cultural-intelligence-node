One record. Six separate operations.

Every operation stays visible at once. A choice around viewing does not silently become storage, transcription, model work or publication.

01

View

Viewing has its own terms.

A person may view material for a named activity while every other operation remains a separate conversation.

02

Store

Storage names custody and duration.

The record identifies working files, backups, logs, temporary copies, who holds them and when review returns.

03

Transcribe

Transcription creates a new record.

Speech, names, language, context and corrections stay connected to contributors and the purpose of the project.

04

Model work

Inputs and outputs stay named.

The project records source material, local processing, model artefacts, access, evaluation and selected outputs.

05

Publish

Publication selects a particular output.

A public film, event listing or model view carries its source, contributors, credits, version and reuse terms.

06

Review

The relationship continues.

People revisit access, purpose, corrections, retention and withdrawal as material and projects develop over time.

Questions that stay attached to material

Who is connected to it?

A file may carry individual, family, organisational, community and cultural relationships at once.

What purpose was agreed?

Viewing, preserving, teaching, transcribing and model training are separate uses.

Where does it live?

Local storage, backups, logs, temporary copies and external services all matter.

Who sees the record?

Access decisions, changes and exports need a history that relevant people understand.

What happens later?

Review dates, withdrawal pathways and deletion limits belong in the first conversation.

What remains human-held?

A project may record a direction or boundary while the knowledge itself stays in living relationship.

These public frameworks add practical questions about collective benefit, control, responsibility, ethics and research relationships. Each project still develops through its own people and context.
