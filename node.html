One shared machine · several local worlds

A GB200-class rack
as an island workbench.

The working example takes the NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack-scale system as a reference point: 72 Blackwell graphics processors, 36 Grace processors and direct liquid cooling. The rack is an example, not the definition of the project.

Possible workload families

One rack, many possible workloads

The same physical system could hold several distinct services without turning them into one model.

Local models

Text, voice, image, video and maps

A family of locally run models can work with public, personal and culturally permissioned material under different rules.

Digital twin

Place, time and systems

The islands can be represented through public maps, environmental measurements, media, infrastructure and chosen cultural layers.

Creative production

Film, sound and virtual worlds

Rendering, editing, restoration, translation, captioning and virtual production can share the same compute foundation.

Research

Simulation and comparison

Environmental, infrastructure, energy, legal and scientific models can be run side by side.

Learning

A visible civic workbench

Students, artists, technicians, researchers and local developers can work with real infrastructure rather than a distant abstraction.

External connection

Clouds without dependency

Outside models and larger systems can remain available while local work retains a home of its own.
