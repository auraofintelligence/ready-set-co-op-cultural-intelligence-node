# Original generated image record

All website hero images and favicon art were generated specifically for this repository on 2 September 2026 using OpenAI's built-in image generation. None was copied, cropped, recoloured or adapted from another Aura of Intelligence project.

## Shared direction

Every hero used a cinematic 2:1 landscape composition with strong subject detail on the right and generous dark space on the left for accessible page headings. The visual language combines contemporary coastal Australia, warm human collaboration, timber and tactile materials with luminous violet, cyan, coral and gold computational layers. People are concept characters rather than depictions of known individuals.

The full negative direction was: no words, labels, logos or watermarks; no windmills or wind turbines; no radio, transmission or communications towers; no antenna masts; no satellite dishes; no lighthouse; no smokestacks; no giant outdoor server monument; no corporate boardroom; no colonial or ceremonial costume; no sacred motifs; no stereotypes; no tokenised cultural symbols; no dystopian surveillance aesthetic.

## Page prompt set

- `assets/heroes/home.webp`: a coastal community studio, Indigenous and non-Indigenous adults gathered around a luminous layered island model, with one real computing rack integrated into the room.
- `assets/heroes/node.webp`: two technicians beside one rack in a quiet timber coastal workspace, showing the machine as a practical workbench rather than a monument.
- `assets/heroes/dunwich.webp`: a colourful contemporary village workshop near a ferry landing after rain, with community life and ordinary island logistics foregrounded.
- `assets/heroes/culture.webp`: a contemporary media studio with Indigenous and non-Indigenous collaborators, layered creative media and a visibly separate protected archive room.
- `assets/heroes/uses.webp`: a lively coastal multi-purpose workbench joining media production, learning, field observation and local computing.
- `assets/heroes/projects.webp`: an intergenerational coastal workshop gathered around one detailed island project table containing sand sport, an outdoor screen, ferry access, media, music, making, materials, energy, wildlife observation and subterranean systems, with one rack nearby and no authority staging.
- `assets/heroes/digital-twin.webp`: a moonlit island model formed from separate translucent data layers above a circular table, with no claim of a total or authoritative view.
- `assets/heroes/process.webp`: an interior planning room where separate coloured workload streams pass between one rack and a layered island model, showing a customisable process.
- `assets/heroes/cooperative.webp`: an intergenerational group sharing technical and media equipment in a warm cooperative workspace, with practical roles and mutual learning visible.
- `assets/heroes/network.webp`: a luminous constellation of distinct island-like intelligence nodes across Oceania and the globe, connected without one dominant centre.
- `assets/heroes/sitemap.webp`: luminous portals distributed across a colourful night-time archipelago, each route distinct and equally available.
- `assets/heroes/not-found.webp`: branching moonlit paths through coastal dunes, inviting a return without warning or blame.
- `assets/favicon.png`: a jewel-like square icon made from several luminous interwoven loops around a dark local centre, with no lettering.

The website uses raster WebP heroes and raster PNG favicons only. Generated source images remain in the local Codex image-output directory; optimised project copies are stored in this repository.
