Repeat the process, not the answer.

The reusable part is a way of asking questions, testing a working version and leaving local choice intact. The equipment, name, relationships, permissions and uses remain different in every place.

Customisable by design

A repeatable plan avoids pretending that Dunwich, another island, a regional town or a cultural organisation needs the same node.

Repeatable
The questions, evidence loop and decision points travel.

Customisable
The purpose, pace, hardware, governance, language and connections stay local.

Reversible
Each stage leaves room to stop, remove, repair or redirect.

Seven movements

01

Listen locally

Gather purposes, concerns, existing projects and points of difference. Record who is present and who is not.

Output: a question map, not a declaration.
02

Map authority and permission

Separate technical control, legal rights, cultural authority, personal consent, organisational responsibility and public material.

Output: permission layers and unresolved boundaries.
03

Choose grounded workloads

Select work people already value or genuinely want to test. Describe inputs, outputs, users, risks and human review.

Output: a defined workload portfolio.
04

Shape the physical node

Match equipment, storage, power, cooling, access, safety and maintenance to those workloads and the place.

Output: a vendor-neutral reference design.
05

Build the people layer

Identify paid roles, training, creative practice, operations, cultural review, maintenance and outside expertise.

Output: a skills and responsibility map.
06

Test a working version

Run grounded pilots and observe real use, energy, time, quality, access and unintended effects.

Output: evidence and corrections.
07

Choose what happens next

Local participants weigh the evidence and choose to stop, adjust, repeat, expand or connect selected parts elsewhere.

Output: a documented local choice.

Four records that travel well

Purpose card

What is being explored, for whom, and what sits outside scope.

Permission map

Material, people, purposes, access, custody, review and withdrawal.

Node sheet

Workloads, equipment, place requirements, skills, costs and recovery.

Evidence log

What was tried, what happened, whose view is recorded and what changed.

Connections come last

A node joins a wider network after its own purposes and boundaries are understood. It may share a method, public output, benchmark, model component or nothing at all. Interoperability is useful only when it does not silently override local protocols.
