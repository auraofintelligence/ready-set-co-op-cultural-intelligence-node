Ready S.E.T. · Sustainable Employment and Training

The cooperative around the rack.

Ready S.E.T. can act as the employment, training, equipment and shared-service layer around a locally held intelligence node. It does not become the owner of every culture, model, dataset or business that connects to it.

The Dunwich placeThe wider network

Working concept by Luke Nathan Hayes. Nothing here speaks for any First Nation, organisation, council or government.

Shared functions

A practical role rather than a giant umbrella

The cooperative can hold the work that becomes easier when people share infrastructure.

People

Paid roles and practical learning

Employment, short training, mentoring, visiting specialists and local pathways can connect to real projects.

Equipment

Shared access and care

Compute, cameras, microphones, screens, sensors, tools and event kits can have clear booking and maintenance records.

Services

Work with visible value

Model hosting, media production, digital-twin work, technical help, research support and local business services can create revenue.

Credentials

Portable proof of work

People may hold their own records of skills, training and contribution rather than depending on one employer database.

Partnerships

Different relationships

A First Nation, family, artist, researcher, business or council can connect through arrangements suited to that work.

Continuity

Capability that stays local

Documentation, spare parts, learning and operational knowledge can remain with people on the island.

Clearer relationships

Cultural authority stays where it already lives

The cooperative may support a cultural business without becoming the cultural authority.

Two value paths

Money and contribution can remain distinct

Paid work stays paid. Voluntary activity may be recognised separately where people choose it.

Market work

Wages, contracts and revenue

Professional operation, cultural production, technical services, energy, maintenance, training and business activity use ordinary money and clear agreements.

Community contribution

Chosen reciprocity

Mentoring, open-source work, environmental care, community events or other freely chosen contribution can carry separate recognition without becoming unpaid employment.
